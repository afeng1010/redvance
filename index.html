Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

1. Introduction

This application ("App") is a Bluetooth remote control tool used to connect and control your own companion hardware device via Bluetooth. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we handle your information.

2. Information We Collect

This App does not collect, upload, or store any personally identifiable information. You do not need to create an account, the App has no user account system, and it does not integrate any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

3. Use of Bluetooth Permission

The Bluetooth permission is requested solely to scan, search, discover, and connect to your Bluetooth hardware device in order to provide the remote control functionality. The App does not use Bluetooth for location tracking and does not access any other data on your device.

4. Local Data Processing

All remote control commands are transmitted locally via Bluetooth on your device. There is no data communication with any cloud server. The App does not access your contacts, photo library, location, microphone, camera, or any other sensitive information.

5. Information Sharing and Disclosure

Because this App does not collect any personal information, there is no possibility of sharing, selling, or disclosing your personal information to any third party.

6. Children's Privacy

This App does not collect any information from children and does not contain content unsuitable for children.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, we will publish the latest version on this page and present it together with future app updates.
